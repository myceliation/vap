Method

Records requests went to New Jersey municipal clerks in two rounds: a hand-sent round in spring 2025 (mostly South Jersey) and an automated round to nearly every municipality in May–June 2026, asking for vacant/abandoned or foreclosed-property registries, unsafe-structure orders, and long-term water shutoffs. Each municipality's dot shows the outcome of its most recent request; outcomes for 2026 were classified from reply text and checked against written denials. Lists that came back as spreadsheets, PDFs or email text were parsed, block/lot was normalized, and each property was placed on the map from the statewide NJOGIS parcel layer (Census geocoder as a fallback). Owner names, contact details and correspondence are left out. Lists are a mix of foreclosure registrations, vacant-property registrations and abandoned-property lists, so treat a listing as a lead, not a fact about today.
